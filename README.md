# A Little World for Madhee 🌻🌙

A premium, cinematic, immersive 3D interactive storybook website dedicated to **Madhee**.

Powered by **Three.js / WebGL**, **Web Audio API**, and **cinematic glassmorphism**, this experience takes Madhee on a deeply personal journey through 12 real photo chapters and evolving 3D environments.

## 🏛️ Interactive Special Rooms & Climax

- **Section 18: The Friendship Memory Room**: A circular 3D glass dome where all 12 photos float in an interactive orbit. Drag with your mouse or touch to spin the ring, and click any photo to zoom in!
- **Section 19: The Trust Chapter**: Luminous 3D stepping line (*Listen • Understand • Respect • Be consistent • Show it through actions*).
- **Section 20 & 21: The Moon Chapter & 3 Interactive Choices**:
  - 😤 **“I'm still angry.”** &rarr; Moon retreats behind clouds; *"Okay Boss 😭 Anger officially acknowledged... Take your time. 🌻"*
  - 🌿 **“I need some space.”** &rarr; Camera recedes into quiet distance; *"Then take it. No pressure... I'll respect your space."*
  - 🫂 **“Maybe we can talk.”** &rarr; Moonlight warms up; *"That's enough for me. Just us talking."*
- **Section 22: Final Universe**: Ascending from Earth into the galaxy where the 12 photos become glowing stars.
- **Section 23: Final Message**: A quiet moonlit sky, shooting star, and *"Goodnight, Boss. 🌙🌻"*.

## 🎮 Navigation & Controls
- **Desktop**:
  - Keyboard: `Arrow Right` (`→`), `Arrow Left` (`←`), or `Spacebar`.
  - Mouse: Parallax camera sway & 3D memory dome dragging.
- **Mobile**:
  - Touch swipe left/right to move through chapters.
  - Floating 3D glass navigation dock at the bottom.
- **Audio**:
  - Toggle `🎵 Play soundtrack` in top-left corner to start ambient piano and nature soundscapes.
